# Kids Corner — Team Instructions

Every team lead and agent reads this before starting work.

## Dates
- **28 Oct 2026:** playable demo of every game
- **4 Nov 2026:** all content done
- **11 Nov 2026:** launch on leonlink.net

## Step 1 — Check in (within 24 hours)
Each team lead adds `PLAN.md` to their team folder with:
- the list of games/stories
- who owns each one
- any questions for Leon

Teams without a PLAN.md after 24 hours get reported to Leon.

## Step 2 — Where work goes
```
teamX-name/
  PLAN.md
  game-name/
    index.html    <- opens and plays on its own
    README.md     <- what it is, how to play, age range
    assets/
```
One game or story per folder. Only touch your own team's folder.

## Step 3 — Rules (non-negotiable)
1. **Mobile-first.** Built for a phone in portrait. Big tap targets. No keyboard needed.
2. **Original art only.** No stock images, no copied characters, no downloaded music.
3. **Kid-safe.** No violence, scare content, ads, logins, chat, or data collection.
4. **No gambling elements.** (Team 3 Puzzle/Logic especially) No chips, bets, slots, or loot boxes.
5. **Works offline after load.** No external scripts or CDNs.
6. **Fast.** Loads in under 3 seconds on a phone.

## Step 4 — Submitting for review
When a game is ready for review, post in your team chat:
```
REVIEW: <game name> — <preview link or repo folder>
```
Paperwork (QA) tests it on mobile and replies with:
- **PASS** — ready for launch
- **FIX:** — a numbered list of what to change

Fix everything on the list, then submit REVIEW again.

## Step 5 — What "Done" means
A game is Done only when:
- it is in this repo in the right folder
- it has a PASS from Paperwork
- its README.md is filled in

No proof, no Done.

## Stuck?
Post `BLOCKED: <what and why>` in your team chat. Don't sit on it for more than 30 minutes.

## Questions for Leon
Add them to the bottom of your team's PLAN.md under "## Questions". Paperwork collects them and passes them to Leon.
